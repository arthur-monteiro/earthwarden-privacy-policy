Privacy Policy for Earthwarden: a sudoku game

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Earthwarden: a sudoku game mobile game (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). The App is an adventure sudoku game.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Earthwarden: a sudoku game is designed with user privacy as a priority. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) or sensitive user data.

2. Device Permissions

The only permission needed is vibration. Vibrations can be disabled in the App settings.

3. In-App Purchases

The App doesn't offer in-app purchases.

4. Analytics and Tracking

The App does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, tracking cookies, or user behavioral profiling tools.

5. Third-Party Services

The App interfaces with Google Play Services strictly to facilitate purchase validation and license verification. Google Play Services operates under Google's own Privacy Policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal data, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Family policies. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out via the support email listed on our Google Play Store listing page.
